Testerify · mobile companion · v1

A phone app for the part of the panel you can't do at your desk: watching tests and shipping winners.

Same warm-paper system as the desktop panel — Instrument Sans, indigo #5b54e0, 16px cards on #f4f1ea. Tap through the phone: the co-pilot generates ideas, tests open to results, and a winner can be shipped end-to-end. Charts stay collapsed until you ask for them. Navigation is four tabs with the co-pilot raised in the centre. iOS shown; on Android the tab bar becomes a Material bottom nav with the co-pilot as a centre-docked FAB.

Navigation — 4 tabs + centre co-pilot Home · Tests · Co-pilot · Alerts · More. The AI sits in the middle as a raised action, so asking for a test is always one thumb-reach away; the remaining 16 sidebar pages live in the More sheet.
